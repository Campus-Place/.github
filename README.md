# Campus Place

> **Connecting student communities through trusted marketplaces**

---

## 🎯 What We Do

Campus Place is building a student-centered marketplace platform that connects students with opportunities, goods, and services within their campus communities.

We focus on accessibility, trust, and community-driven commerce.

---

## 💡 Our Approach

### Community First

We treat students as ecosystem participants who create marketplace value through their activity and engagement.

### Accessibility Matters

Students navigate their world based on accessibility and convenience, not just distance. Our platform reflects that reality.

### Trust Always

We prioritize safety, verification, and accountability in everything we build.
